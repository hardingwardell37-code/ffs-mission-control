# Craft — high-end photoreal ads

Read with the matching example open. Treatment follows the mode; originality and DNA still win.

## Hierarchy

- **One dominant headline** — largest type, highest contrast, not competing with the logo.
- **Product or person as focal** — size, isolation, light, or shallow DOF; one clear subject.
- **CTA as a discrete control** — filled pill, rounded rectangle, or solid bar; never floating body text alone when an ad was requested.
- Cap loud elements at three: headline · hero · CTA. Never equal weight on logo / product / copy.

## Type

- **Large display** — serif **or** bold sans for the lead line (editorial examples use large brand/display type behind or beside the subject; series frames use bold white sans on color fields).
- **Quiet support** — smaller subhead, caption, or footer line; clear step down in size/weight.
- Quote every drawn string exactly. Do not invent statistics, ratings, or badges.

## Color

- **Limited palette** — monochrome/neutrals (editorial), one strong field color per series frame, or pack-matched environment color (product system).
- **One accent** for CTA and graphic overlays (pill, speech-bubble fill, footer bar) — held small so it pulls.

## Photography

- **Soft studio or natural light** — one coherent key; real contact shadow.
- **Shallow DOF** where lifestyle / people appear.
- **Reflective surfaces** for luxury glass and metal (highlights, rim light, honest refraction) — never plastic CGI sheen.
- Skin, fabric, and pack materials named with real texture (pores, weave, satin glass) — **FAIL** cartoon / CGI plastic.

## Series and systems

- **Repeat** logo placement, footer lock, and CTA treatment across every frame in a set.
- Branded series: thick brand-color border/field; consistent logo + footer CTA; photoreal people; optional speech-bubble / graphic overlays that do not flatten hierarchy.
- Product system: pack color matches environment; footer lock (logo + tagline + URL); mix lifestyle + hero + family without breaking the lock.
- Luxury board → end card: short tagline on the final static frame; hold product identity across panels.

## Cinematic outdoor hero

Mode `cinematic-outdoor-hero` (example 05). Product owns the frame in a real environment — not a packshot on seamless.

- **Hierarchy** — small product/line lockup → large serif or display headline in sky/negative space → quiet feature/spec bar → outline or boxed CTA + URL below. Product is the visual weight; type steps down cleanly.
- **Wet ground reflections** — damp stone/pavement mirrors headlights, warm practicals, and cool sky; keep reflections sharp and grounded, not CGI gloss.
- **Cool ambient + warm fill** — blue-hour / dusk ambient with warm practicals from architecture or interior light; one coherent key, honest rim on metal/glass.
- **CTA treatment** — thin outline / boxed rectangle (not a filled pill); URL sits under the box in quiet sans. Prefer outline when the photo is already dramatic.
- **Safe margins** — headline, feature bar, boxed CTA, and URL clear of edges and platform chrome; leave breathing room in the negative-space sky band.

## Aspect and margins

- Generate the **true aspect** the brief asks for (default **1:1** feed unless told; storyboard end cards may be **9:16** when briefed).
- Keep headline, product lockup, and CTA inside **safe margins** — clear of edges and platform chrome.

## Negatives (reject)

- Blank / unlabeled packs
- Melted, warped, or gibberish type
- Stock HDR glow / multi-source catalogue lighting
- Cluttered equal-weight collage
- Missing CTA when an ad was requested
- Bare packshot when finished-ad structure was requested
- Cartoon / CGI plastic materials or skin

## Prompt assembly (summary)

1. Preservation — locked product unchanged when refs attached  
2. Layout — mode from example; regions for headline / hero / CTA; aspect + margins  
3. Copy — quoted strings from brief/DNA only  
4. Look — photoreal light + materials; limited palette + one accent  
5. Negatives — list above
