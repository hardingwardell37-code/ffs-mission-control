## What this tree is

A **lean** pull of three strategy/copy skills for F&P Studio portfolio campaign work:

- `product-marketing` (foundation context other marketing skills expect)
- `ad-creative` (ad copy, templates, creative strategy — plus `references/` and the creative-review HTML asset)
- `copywriting` (page/landing conversion copy — plus `references/`)

It is **not** a dump of the full ~50-skill upstream catalogue. Upstream evals, tools CLIs, and sibling skills (`ads`, `copy-editing`, `marketing-loops`, etc.) are **omitted on purpose**.

## F&P usage note

These skills are **strategy and copy frameworks**. They do **not** replace F&P Generate craft:

- Finished photoreal statics → [`high-end-photoreal-ads/`](high-end-photoreal-ads/) + [`ORIGINALITY.md`](ORIGINALITY.md)
- AdScoop / Meta Library research → [`adscoop-to-fp-handoff/`](adscoop-to-fp-handoff/)
- Do **not** weaken originality: patterns and frameworks only; invent original packs/lines grounded in Campaign DNA.
