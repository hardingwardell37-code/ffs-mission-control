## What this tree is

A **lean, originality-guarded** adaptation of three creative skills for F&P Studio portfolio campaign work:

- `generating-product-photos`
- `generating-image-ads`
- `generating-ad-videos`

It is **not** a vendored copy of the SuperCMO monorepo. Upstream MCP tools (`image_generate`, `video_generate`, etc.) are rewritten here as **F&P Generate** provider-ready prompts + campaign asset registration.

## Omitted on purpose

- `cloning-video-ads` and any skill that encourages copying competitor ads
- The rest of the SuperCMO skills catalogue (UGC, cartoon, competitor research pipelines, etc.)
- Upstream evals, scripts, fonts, and private MCP wiring
